About HelpNearby

A community-driven platform that helps people in emergencies by connecting them with nearby registered volunteers.

Our Mission

To build a safer community by ensuring that help is always nearby, when it is needed most.

Safety Guidelines

  • Verify information before sharing.
  • Do not take risks while helping.
  • In case of severe emergency, call official services.
  • This platform is NOT a replacement for emergency services.
